Privacy Policy

Pickle Players · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Pickle Players ("the app") helps you randomly assign pickleball players to courts. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

The only information the app stores is the list of player names you type in, so they are still there the next time you open the app. These names are saved locally on your device. Court assignments are kept only while the app is running.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not request internet access and does not send any data off your device. It has no accounts or sign-in, and no advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Sharing

Because no data leaves your device, nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.

Device backups

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Android may include the app's saved player names in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup) so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is managed by Android and your Google account settings, not by the app, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

Deleting your data

You can remove any player by tapping the ✕ next to their name. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
